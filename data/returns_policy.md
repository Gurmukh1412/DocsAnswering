# RETURNS POLICY

Effective Date: February 1, 2026  
Company: Generic Commerce Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

## 1. RETURNS WINDOW
Customers may return physical goods within 14 days of delivery.

## 2. CONDITION REQUIREMENTS
Returned items must:
- Be unused
- Include all original accessories
- Be in original packaging

## 3. RETURN SHIPPING
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless:
- The product is defective
- The wrong product was delivered

## 4. DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
If a product arrives damaged:
- Notify customer support within 48 hours
- Provide photos as proof

## 5. INTERNATIONAL RETURNS
International returns are accepted; however:
- Customers must pay international shipping fees.
- Customs duties are non-refundable.

## 6. EXCHANGES
Exchanges are allowed for defective or damaged items only.
