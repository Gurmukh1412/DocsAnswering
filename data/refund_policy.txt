REFUND POLICY
Effective Date: February 1, 2026
Company: Generic Commerce Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

1. OVERVIEW
This Refund Policy outlines the terms under which customers may request and receive refunds for products purchased from Generic Commerce Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

2. REFUND ELIGIBILITY
Customers may request a refund within 14 calendar days from the date of delivery of the product.

To be eligible for a refund:
- The item must be unused and in original condition.
- The item must be returned in original packaging.
- Proof of purchase must be provided.

3. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS
The following items are not eligible for refund:
- Digital downloads
- Gift cards
- Customized or personalized products

4. REFUND PROCESS
Once the returned item is received and inspected:
- Customers will be notified via email.
- If approved, refund will be processed within 5–7 business days.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

5. LATE OR MISSING REFUNDS
If you have not received a refund within 10 business days:
- Check your bank account.
- Contact your credit card company.
- Contact our support team.

6. INTERNATIONAL ORDERS
Refunds for international orders are subject to customs clearance policies and may take additional time.

7. PARTIAL REFUNDS
In some situations, partial refunds may be granted:
- Items returned with minor damage.
- Items returned missing minor components.
