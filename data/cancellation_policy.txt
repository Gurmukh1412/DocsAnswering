CANCELLATION POLICY
Effective Date: February 1, 2026
Company: Generic Commerce Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

1. ORDER CANCELLATION
Customers may cancel an order before it has been dispatched from our warehouse.

2. POST-DISPATCH CANCELLATION
Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. Customers must follow the Returns Policy to request a refund.

3. SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES
Subscription-based services may be cancelled at any time.
However:
- Cancellation becomes effective at the end of the current billing cycle.
- No partial refunds for subscription periods already billed.

4. AUTOMATIC CANCELLATION
Orders may be automatically cancelled if:
- Payment authorization fails.
- Product is out of stock.
- Suspected fraudulent activity.

5. REFUND AFTER CANCELLATION
If cancellation occurs before dispatch, a full refund will be processed within 5 business days.
